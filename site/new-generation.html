An open letter to President Obama

Dear President Obama,

This year you crossed the country for a new generation of Democrats. In Austin, in Detroit, in Georgia, you stood beside a teacher, a doctor and a journalist and told people they were worth believing in. On November 3, voters agreed.

We are ordinary Americans from every state who have started a grassroots movement asking one of them, Senator Jon Ossoff, to run for President in 2028. He has said he isn't planning to. We respect that, and we're asking anyway, because we think the country needs what he, Senator-elect Talarico and Senator-elect El-Sayed represent: leaders who take on corruption in both parties, who win in hard places, and who talk to voters as grown-ups.

We're not asking you to choose a candidate. We know that's not your role before the voters speak. We're asking you to keep doing what you did this year: lift up this generation, together, where the country can see them. A picture of three new senators who beat the odds, standing side by side with the man who once beat the odds himself, would tell millions of discouraged Americans that the future is worth showing up for.

Every name below belongs to someone who believes it.

— The Draft Ossoff 2028 volunteers and our co-signers
