Volunteer code of conduct

We speak for ourselves

Never claim to represent Sen. Ossoff, his office or any campaign, and never contact them as if you do.

Facts with sources

Everything we publish has a source. No rumors, no edited photos, no made-up quotes.

Civil, always

We argue with ideas, not people. No harassment of anyone, including opponents and their staff.

Protect people's data

Supporter emails and details stay inside the core team. Never export, share or sell them.

Least access

You get access to what your role needs, nothing more. Accounts are owned by the movement, not individuals.

No money in, no money out

Volunteers never pay for things on the movement's behalf without a written OK from the core team, and never take donations.
