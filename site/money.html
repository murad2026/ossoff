Our money pledge

Clean money starts with no money.

A movement against corruption has to be beyond question on money. So we keep it simple: we don't raise it, we don't take it, and nobody here gets paid.

Five promises

  • We never ask for donationsNo “chip in $5”, no fundraising emails, no crypto wallets, no payment links. Anywhere.
  • We don't accept moneyNot from individuals, companies, PACs, campaigns or anyone outside the United States. If someone offers, we say no.
  • Nobody is paidEvery organizer, moderator and volunteer works unpaid. Volunteers give time, never money.
  • We buy no adsWe grow by people sharing in their own words, not by paid promotion.
  • We'll tell you firstIf that ever changes, we will form a proper organization, register and report as federal election law requires, and publish it here before a single dollar moves.
