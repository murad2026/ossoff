Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

The short version:

We collect only what the movement needs: your answer, your story, and an email if you choose to give one.

We publish only your first name, state and the message you approve.

We never sell, rent or trade your data, and we never give it to any campaign, party or PAC.

Email us and we'll delete your data.

Who we are

Draft Ossoff 2028 is an unofficial, volunteer-run grassroots website at draftossoff.org. We are not affiliated with Sen. Jon Ossoff, his office or any campaign. Questions: .

What we collect and why

We do not use advertising trackers, analytics cookies or tracking pixels.

What is public

Only approved stories appear on the site, showing your first name, state, message and tags. Your email and everything in a volunteer application stay private.

Services that process data for us

  • Cloudflare runs our server and database.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website files. Like any web host, it may log visitors' network addresses.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) receives the text of your message to check it for civility and suggest an edit. It does not receive your name or email.
  • Google Fonts delivers the site's typefaces, so your browser contacts Google when pages load.

Each of these services handles data under its own privacy policy.

What we never do

  • Sell, rent, trade or give away your data.
  • Share it with any campaign, candidate, party, PAC or political organization.
  • Ask you for money or payment details. See our $0 pledge.

Who inside the movement can see it

Moderators see stories waiting for review: first name, state and message, but not emails. Only the site owner and admins can see volunteer applications. Every moderation action is logged.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to delete it, or until the movement ends, whichever comes first. Rate-limit records are short-lived and cleared regularly. When the movement ends, we will delete supporter data rather than pass it on.

Your choices

  • Delete: email with the address you used, and we will remove your story, signup and any volunteer application.
  • Unsubscribe: every update we send will include a way to stop receiving them.
  • Correct: ask us to fix anything we got wrong.

Age

The site is meant for people 13 and older. Please don't post or apply if you're younger.

Security

Data is stored with access limited to the core team, admin access is protected by personal tokens, and network addresses are stored only in scrambled form. No system is perfect. If we ever learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If we ever change how we use data you already gave us, we'll ask first.
