Why Jon

Facts from the public record. Sources at the bottom of the page.

Took on congressional stock trading

Wrote the Ban Congressional Stock Trading Act with Sen. Mark Kelly and put his own portfolio in a blind trust. In 2024 a bipartisan version became the first such ban to pass a Senate committee.

Gets laws passed

Passed more standalone bills into law in his first two years than any other freshman senator — most with Republican partners.

Investigates abuse

As chair of the Permanent Subcommittee on Investigations he probed federal prisons, military family housing and immigration detention.

Backed the bipartisan border deal

Supported the 2024 bipartisan border security bill and kept pressing both parties to pass it after it was blocked.

Stands with allies

Voted to advance the 2024 national security package with aid for Ukraine, Israel and Taiwan.

Journalist turned senator

Born in Georgia in 1987. Elected in 2021 as the youngest sitting U.S. senator and re-elected in 2026.

How we moderate

Tone, not views

Civil disagreement stays up. Insults, threats, hate, spam and fake quotes come down — whoever posts them.

AI checks, people decide

An AI flags problems and suggests edits. Anything borderline goes to a volunteer moderator, and every decision is logged.

You can appeal

If your post is turned down, you'll see why and can ask a human to look again.

Your data stays yours

Emails are private and never sold. We publish only your first name, state and message.
